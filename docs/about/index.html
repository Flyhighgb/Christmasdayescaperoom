About Christmas Escape Room

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

Christmas Escape Room (christmasescaperoom.co.uk) is a free UK website of Christmas escape room games you can play online or print at home, plus tools for making your own.

Who makes the games

I’m Paul, the creator of Christmas Escape Room. I write and test every puzzle on the site, from the picture clues for toddlers to the Christmas Carol cipher hunt for adults, and I keep the games free so any family, school, care home or office can use them.

Why I made it

Most Christmas escape rooms online are paid downloads or classroom packs. I wanted a place where any family, teacher, care home or office could find a good festive puzzle game in seconds, for free, with no sign-up.

How the games are made

  • Every game has a full answer key and a hint for each clue.
  • Each game page lists the age range, player count, play time and prep time, so you can choose quickly.
  • We use UK spelling and British Christmas traditions: crackers, mince pies, Boxing Day.
  • No game needs padlocks or props you’d have to buy.

Using our games

You’re welcome to use any game at home, in school, in a care home or at a work party. Please link to the page rather than re-uploading the files elsewhere.

Contact

Ideas for a new game, or spotted a mistake? Email Paul at hello@christmasescaperoom.co.uk.
