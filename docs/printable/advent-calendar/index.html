Christmas escape room advent calendar: 24 free printable puzzles

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

This free printable advent calendar gives children one tiny puzzle a day from 1 to 24 December. Each puzzle reveals one letter, and on Christmas Eve the 24 letters spell a secret festive message. Each day takes about 2 minutes and suits ages 5–9.

Ages5–9
Players1–4
TimeAbout 2 minutes a day, 1–24 December
Set-upPrint one page
Puzzles24: number codes, alphabet order and missing letters
You needPrinter, pencil; scissors optional

If nothing happened, press Ctrl + P or ⌘ + P.

How it works

  1. Print the calendar and pin it to the fridge.
  2. Each morning, solve that day’s puzzle and write the letter in the box.
  3. On 24 December, read all 24 letters in order to reveal the secret message.

Frequently asked questions

What is an escape room advent calendar?

It’s one small puzzle a day from 1 to 24 December. Each puzzle gives one letter, and on Christmas Eve the 24 letters spell a secret festive message.

What age is it for?

Ages 5–9 can do it with a little help; it takes about 2 minutes a day. Older children can race to solve it before the 24th.

How do I use it?

Print the page, cut out each day (or fold it) and reveal one a day, or keep the sheet on the fridge and fill in one box each morning.
