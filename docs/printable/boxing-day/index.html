Boxing Day escape room: a printable game for the whole family

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

The Leftovers Lockdown is a free printable Boxing Day escape room for ages 8 to adult. Grandad has locked the chocolate tin; players crack a riddle, a Caesar code, a pigpen cipher and a Boxing Day sales maths lock to get it back. It takes 30–45 minutes for 2–8 players.

Players2–8

Answer key

If nothing happened, press Ctrl + P (Windows) or ⌘ + P (Mac). Each card prints on its own page; host notes don’t print.

The story

It’s Boxing Day. Everyone is full of turkey, but Grandad has locked the last tin of chocolates away ‘to stop you lot eating it all’. He’s left a trail of clues through the leftovers, the board games and the coat hooks. Solve them to win the tin.

What’s in the game

  1. Grandad’s note: a riddle about leftovers.
  2. The family tradition: a Caesar shift of two.
  3. Time for a walk: a pigpen cipher with key.
  4. The Boxing Day sales: a percentages maths lock.

How to set it up

  1. Print the cards below. Each card prints on its own sheet and the host notes are left off.
  2. Hide each card in the place shown in its host note and in the answer key. Start from the last card and work backwards.
  3. Put the final card with the chocolate tin. Lock it with a 3-digit padlock set to 036, or have the host check the code.
  4. Start the escape room timer and hand Card 1 to the players.

Answer key

Card 1BREAD BINRiddle. Card 2 is in or by the bread bin.
Card 2BOARD GAMESShift each letter back two. Card 3 is in the board games cupboard or a game box.
Card 3COATSPigpen cipher. Card 4 is in a coat pocket on the coat hooks.
Card 4036£40 − 25% = £30; £12 ÷ 2 = £6; total £36 → 036. It opens the chocolate tin.

Make it easier or harder

  • Easier: tell players that card 2 moves letters back two, and let them use a calculator for the sales.
  • Harder: remove the pigpen key and hide it under the Christmas tree, and set a 25-minute limit.

Frequently asked questions

What can families do on Boxing Day?

A Boxing Day escape room gets everyone off the sofa without leaving the house. The Leftovers Lockdown takes 30–45 minutes, suits ages 8 to adult, and ends with a tin of chocolates.

Do I need a padlock?

No. A 3-digit padlock set to 036 adds drama, but the host can simply check the answer.

Can grandparents play?

Yes. The puzzles use leftovers, board games and the Boxing Day sales, so every generation can help.

Is there an answer key?

Yes, at the bottom of this page.
