KS2 Christmas escape room: free printable classroom game

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

The Elf Inspection is a free Christmas escape room for KS2 classes (Years 3–6). Teams crack a times-tables code, an adjectives puzzle and a fractions and multiplication lock to pass Santa’s elf inspection. It takes 45–60 minutes for teams of 4–6, with a teacher answer key.

Year groupsKS2: Years 3–6 (ages 7–11)
Teams4–6 pupils per team; print one set per team
Time45–60 minutes

Answer key

The story

Santa’s Head Elf is visiting your class to see who is clever enough to work in the toy workshop. Each team must pass three stations, maths, English and the toy count, to earn its elf badges before the end of the lesson.

What’s in the game

  1. Station 1: The times tables code: times tables and division, turned into letters.
  2. Station 2: Adjective detectives: an English grammar puzzle (adjectives).
  3. Station 3: The toy count: fractions and multiplication word problems.

How to set it up

  1. Print the cards below. Each card prints on its own sheet and the host notes are left off.
  2. Hide each card in the place shown in its host note and in the answer key. Start from the last card and work backwards.
  3. Keep the final card (one per team) and hand it over when a team gives all three answers. Print certificates with our certificate maker.
  4. Start the escape room timer and hand Card 1 to the players.

Make it easier or harder

  • Easier: allow a multiplication grid and read the word problems aloud (Year 3–4).
  • Harder: set a 30-minute limit and remove the A–Z table (Year 6).

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free KS2 Christmas escape room?

Yes. The Elf Inspection is free for Years 3–6, takes 45–60 minutes and covers times tables, division, adjectives and fractions, with a teacher answer key.

How do I run it with a whole class?

Split the class into teams of 4–6 and print one set per team. Either give each team Station 1 and hand out the next station when they show you the right answer, or set up three tables as stations and rotate.

Which year groups is it for?

Years 3–6. For Year 3, read the questions aloud and allow a times-table grid; Year 6 can play with a 30-minute limit.

Does it link to the curriculum?

Yes: multiplication and division facts, fractions of amounts, word problems and identifying adjectives, all part of the KS2 maths and English programmes of study.
