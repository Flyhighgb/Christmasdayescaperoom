Christmas Eve escape room: a printable bedtime game for families

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

The Night Before Christmas Mission is a free printable Christmas Eve escape room for ages 5–10. Children help Santa get the house ready (pyjamas, Rudolph’s carrot, Santa’s mince pie) and the final clue is hidden under their pillow, so the game ends at bedtime. It takes 20–30 minutes.

Ages5–10
Players1–6
Time20–30 minutes
Set-up10 minutes
DifficultyEasy
Best timeChristmas Eve, before bed
You needPrinter, 6 sheets of A4, a carrot, a mince pie, a small Christmas Eve gift (optional)
Puzzles4 + final card
Answer key

If nothing happened, press Ctrl + P (Windows) or ⌘ + P (Mac). Each card prints on its own page; host notes don’t print.

The story

Santa has sent a letter: before he can visit, the children must complete four Christmas Eve jobs. Each job leads to the next clue: into their pyjamas, out with Rudolph’s carrot, a mince pie for Santa, and finally into bed, where Santa’s ‘Nice List’ certificate is waiting under the pillow.

What’s in the game

  1. A letter from Santa: an A=1 number code inside Santa’s letter.
  2. Rudolph is hungry: a missing-letters puzzle.
  3. Santa’s favourite snack: a backwards message.
  4. Santa’s bedtime poem: an acrostic poem.

How to set it up

  1. Print the cards below. Each card prints on its own sheet and the host notes are left off.
  2. Hide each card in the place shown in its host note and in the answer key. Start from the last card and work backwards.
  3. Put the final card, and a small Christmas Eve gift if you like, under the children’s pillow.
  4. Start the escape room timer and hand Card 1 to the players.

Make it easier or harder

  • Easier: read each card aloud and help with the number code using the A–Z table.
  • Harder: cover the A–Z table, and hide each card more carefully.

Frequently asked questions

What can we do on Christmas Eve with kids?

A Christmas Eve escape room is a fun way to calm the excitement: The Night Before Christmas Mission takes 20–30 minutes and leads children through putting on pyjamas, leaving out Rudolph’s carrot and Santa’s mince pie, and ends with the last clue under their pillow.

What age is it for?

Ages 5–10. Children aged 5–6 may need help with the number code; older children can play on their own.

When should we play it?

About an hour before bed on Christmas Eve, so it ends with everyone in pyjamas and ready to sleep.

Can I use it with a Christmas Eve box?

Yes. Put the Christmas Eve box under the pillow with the final card for a lovely finish.
