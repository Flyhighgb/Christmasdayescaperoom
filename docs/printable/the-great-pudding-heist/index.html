The Great Pudding Heist: free printable Christmas escape room

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

The Great Pudding Heist is a free printable Christmas escape room for ages 6 and up. Mischief the elf has hidden the Christmas pudding; players follow five clue cards hidden around the house to find it. It takes 10 minutes to set up and 30–45 minutes to play, with no padlocks needed.

Ages6+ (4–5 with help)
Players2–8
Time30–45 minutes
Set-up10 minutes
DifficultyEasy
You needPrinter, 6 sheets of A4, pen, a prize (optional)

Answer key

If nothing happened, press Ctrl + P (Windows) or ⌘ + P (Mac). Each card prints on its own page; host notes don’t print.

The story

Mischief, the cheekiest elf at the North Pole, has hidden the family’s Christmas pudding just before dinner. He’s left a trail of clues around the house, each one harder than the last. Players must follow the trail from the fridge to the stocking, the sofa and the cracker box to find the pudding in time.

What’s in the game

  1. The letter: a rhyming riddle inside Mischief’s letter.
  2. The scramble: a word scramble.
  3. The number code: an A=1 number code.
  4. The shifty code: a Caesar shift of one.
  5. The backwards message: a backwards message.

How to set it up

  1. Print the cards below. Each card prints on its own sheet and the host notes are left off.
  2. Hide each card in the place shown in its host note and in the answer key. Start from the last card and work backwards.
  3. Put the pudding (or a prize) and the final card in a kitchen cupboard.
  4. Start the escape room timer and hand Card 1 to the players.

Card 1

The letter

Give this to the players to start.

Dear detectives,

I’m Mischief, the cheekiest elf at the North Pole, and I’ve hidden your Christmas pudding! Ha!

Follow my clues to find it before Christmas dinner. Here’s the first one:

I hum all day and I’m cold inside,
where the milk and butter like to hide.

Love (not really),
Mischief the Elf

Card 4

The shifty code

Mischief moved every letter ONE place forward. Move each one back to read it.

D S B D L F S T

Example: D becomes C.

🧑‍🎄 Host: Under a sofa cushion.

Card 5

The backwards message

Mischief wrote this clue back to front. Read it from right to left.

D R A O B P U C   N E H C T I K

That’s where the pudding is!

🧑‍🎄 Host: In or under the box of Christmas crackers.

You found the pudding!

Make it easier or harder

  • Easier: read each card aloud and leave a corner poking out of each hiding place.
  • Harder: set a 20-minute limit, cover the hints with sticky notes and put a combination padlock (code 133) on the prize.

Frequently asked questions

What do I need for The Great Pudding Heist?

A printer, 6 sheets of A4 paper, a pen and the hiding places: fridge, a stocking, the sofa, a box of crackers and a kitchen cupboard.

How long does it take?

Set-up takes about 10 minutes. Most families finish the trail in 30–45 minutes.

Can I play it without a printer?

Yes. Copy the five clues by hand onto paper, or play our online game The Missing Sleigh Key, which needs no printer.

What if I don’t have one of the hiding places?

Swap it for one you have and change the clue to match. Use our cipher maker to re-code the new word.

Is it suitable for adults?

Yes. Add a 20-minute limit and remove the hints. For a properly hard adult game, try Scrooge’s Safe.
