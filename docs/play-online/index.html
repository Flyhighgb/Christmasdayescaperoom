Frequently asked questions

Do I need to download anything to play?

No. Our online Christmas escape rooms run in any modern web browser on a phone, tablet or computer. There’s nothing to install and no account to make.

Can we play together over Zoom or FaceTime?

Yes. One person shares their screen and everyone shouts out answers, or each household opens the same game and races to finish first.

Can I use it at an office Christmas party?

Yes. Split into teams of 2–6, give each team the link and compare finishing times. It takes about 20–30 minutes and costs nothing.
