No printer needed

Christmas escape room without a printer

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

You can run a Christmas escape room with no printer in two ways: play a free online game such as The Missing Sleigh Key on any phone or laptop, or copy the four short clues below onto paper by hand. The handwritten game takes 5 minutes to set up and 15–20 minutes to play.

Option 2: write the clues by hand

  1. Copy each clue below onto its own scrap of paper.
  2. Hide each one where the host note says.
  3. Put a small prize under the Christmas tree.
  4. Hand clue 1 to the players and start the timer.
Clue 1 (give this to the players)

I have a face and two hands, but no arms or legs. I tell you when it’s bedtime.

🧑‍🎄 Host: Put clue 2 next to a clock. Answer: CLOCK

Clue 2 (next to a clock)

Backwards alphabet code: Z = 1, Y = 2, X = 3 … A = 26.
8 · 12 · 21 · 26

🧑‍🎄 Host: Put clue 3 under a sofa cushion. Answer: SOFA

Clue 3 (under a sofa cushion)

Take the first letter of each word: Tinsel, Angel, Bauble, Lights, Elf.

🧑‍🎄 Host: Put clue 4 under the table. Answer: TABLE

Clue 4 (under the table)

Last one! Where does Santa leave the presents? Look under the ____.

🧑‍🎄 Host: Put the prize under the Christmas tree. Answer: TREE

Frequently asked questions

Can you do a Christmas escape room without a printer?

Yes. Either play one online, like The Missing Sleigh Key, on a phone or laptop, or copy four short clues onto scraps of paper by hand. Both take under 10 minutes to set up.

What do I need for the handwritten version?

Four pieces of paper, a pen, and a small prize to hide under the Christmas tree.

How long does it take?

About 5 minutes to write the clues and 15–20 minutes to play.
