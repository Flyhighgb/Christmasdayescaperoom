Reindeer Rescue: free virtual Christmas team game for Teams and Zoom

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

Reindeer Rescue: Team Race is a free Christmas team game for work parties on Microsoft Teams, Zoom or in the office. Teams of 2–6 race through six reindeer puzzles in 20–30 minutes, paste a finish code into the meeting chat, and the host’s free leaderboard ranks every team. Any number of teams can play, with no sign-up and no booking.

Ready to rescue the reindeer?

Santa’s reindeer have escaped the night before Christmas Eve. Your team has six puzzles to round them up. Enter the room code from your host (leave it blank to play just for fun) and your team name, then wait for the host to say “Go!”

How to run it on Microsoft Teams

  1. Host: open the leaderboard page and copy the join message. It includes your 4-letter room code.
  2. Paste the join message into the Teams meeting chat and split everyone into breakout rooms of 2–6.
  3. In each breakout room, one person opens the link and shares their screen while the team solves the puzzles together.
  4. Say “Go!”. When a team finishes, they paste their finish code into the main meeting chat.
  5. Bring everyone back, copy the whole meeting chat into the leaderboard page and press Show results. Share your screen for the podium.
On Zoom or Google Meet it works the same way. In the office, give each table a laptop and ask teams to message or read out their codes.

Want it pinned in a Teams channel? Select + on the channel’s tab bar, choose Website, and paste this page’s address. Some company setups open website tabs in a browser window, which works just as well.

What’s in the game

  1. The runaway: an anagram of a reindeer’s name.
  2. The name tags: a counting puzzle.
  3. Hoofprints in the snow: a grid-and-directions puzzle.
  4. The coded sign: a Caesar shift.
  5. Who’s in stall 1?: a logic puzzle.
  6. The stable lock: a final code using earlier answers.

Puzzles build on each other: teams need answers from earlier puzzles to open the final lock, so everyone has to work together.

Answers (for hosts)

  1. Puzzle 1: BLITZEN (anagram of TNBZILE.)
  2. Puzzle 2: 47 (6+6+7+5+5+5+6+7 = 47.)
  3. Puzzle 3: LICHEN (C3 L, C1 I, E1 C, E2 H, B2 E, B5 N.)
  4. Puzzle 4: STABLE (shift each letter back 8.)
  5. Puzzle 5: DANCER (Comet 3, Cupid 2, Dasher 5, Vixen 4, Dancer 1.)
  6. Puzzle 6: 447 (Vixen’s stall 4, then 47.)

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free Christmas team game for Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Reindeer Rescue: Team Race is free and made for Teams and Zoom. The host shares a 4-letter room code, teams play in breakout rooms on their own laptops, and each team pastes its finish code into the meeting chat. The host pastes the chat into the free leaderboard to rank every team.

How many teams can play?

As many as you like. There’s no server and no sign-up, so 3 teams or 300 teams works the same.

How long does it take?

Most teams finish in 20–30 minutes, so with instructions and results it fits a 45-minute slot.

Do players need to install anything or sign up?

No. Everyone just opens the link in a browser on a laptop, tablet or phone.

Can teams cheat?

Each finish code includes a check, so a typed-in or edited time is rejected by the leaderboard. Codes only count for the room code the host shares, so old codes can’t be reused.

Does it work in the office, without Teams?

Yes. Give each table a laptop, start together, and have teams read or message their finish codes to the host.

Is it free for companies?

Yes, it’s free for any office, school or club. A link back to the site is always appreciated.
