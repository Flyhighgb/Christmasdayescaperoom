The elves are locking up in 10 minutes!

You’ve been accidentally locked in Santa’s workshop. Solve three quick puzzles to get out before the elves go home for Christmas.

How to play

  1. Press Start the game.
  2. Solve each puzzle and press Unlock.
  3. Try to finish in under 10 minutes!

What’s in the game

  1. The jumbled toy: an anagram.
  2. The doubling elves: a number pattern.
  3. The door label: a Caesar shift of one.

Answers

Only peek if you’re really stuck.

  1. Puzzle 1: SNOWMAN – anagram of NOTSWAM.
  2. Puzzle 2: 48 – 24 × 2.
  3. Puzzle 3: TINSEL – shift each letter back one.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a quick Christmas escape room?

Yes. The 10-Minute Workshop Escape has three puzzles and takes about 10 minutes online, so it fits a lesson starter, a work break or the gap between courses.

Can I use it in class?

Yes. Put it on the interactive whiteboard and let the class solve it together, or let pairs play on tablets.

Want something longer?

Try The Missing Sleigh Key (20–30 minutes online) or a printable game such as The Great Pudding Heist.
