The Office Party Escape is a free online Christmas escape room for work teams. Split into teams of 2–6, open the link on a laptop or over Zoom or Teams, and race to crack five office-themed puzzles in 20–30 minutes. No host, booking or payment needed.

It’s 4.55pm on the last day before Christmas…

The office party is about to start, but someone has locked the meeting room where the mince pies are kept. Your team has five clues left by the office elf. Get in before the pies are gone.

How to play

  1. Split into teams of 2–6 and send each team this page’s link.
  2. Everyone presses Start at the same moment.
  3. Each team notes its time and hints used when it escapes.
  4. Fastest time wins (add 2 minutes per hint). Print a certificate for the winners.

What’s in the game

  1. The inbox: an acrostic hidden in email subject lines.
  2. The desk phone: a phone keypad (multi-tap) code.
  3. The spreadsheet: spreadsheet cell references.
  4. The food rota: a short logic puzzle.
  5. The meeting room keypad: a code built from the answers so far.

Hosting tips for work parties

  • In the office: one laptop per team, timer on the meeting room screen.
  • Hybrid or remote: breakout rooms of 3–5 people, one person shares their screen.
  • Big groups (30+): run it as a race in rounds and play the final between the two fastest teams.
  • Prizes: first choice from the Secret Santa pile goes down well.

Answers

Only peek if you’re really stuck.

  1. Puzzle 1: PARTY – first letters of the subject lines.
  2. Puzzle 2: SANTA – multi-tap phone code.
  3. Puzzle 3: MINCE – letters at B2, D4, A1, E5, C3.
  4. Puzzle 4: BEN – Amy turkey, Cat crackers, Dev sprouts, Ben pudding.
  5. Puzzle 5: 5553 – PARTY 5, SANTA 5, MINCE 5, BEN 3.

Frequently asked questions

How do we run an office Christmas escape room for free?

Split everyone into teams of 2–6, send each team the link to this page, start at the same time and compare finishing times. Put the escape room timer on the big screen for atmosphere. There’s no booking, host or cost.

Does it work for remote teams on Zoom or Teams?

Yes. Use breakout rooms: each team shares one screen and works together, then everyone returns to the main room to compare times and hints used.

How long does it take?

Most teams finish in 20–30 minutes, which fits a lunchtime or the start of a party.

Is it suitable for everyone in the office?

Yes. The puzzles use everyday office things (emails, phones, spreadsheets) and need no Christmas knowledge beyond the basics. Nothing is religious or rude.

How do we pick a winner?

Fastest time wins. Add 2 minutes for each hint used, and print certificates for the winning team with our certificate maker.
