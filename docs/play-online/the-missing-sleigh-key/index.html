The Missing Sleigh Key: free online Christmas escape room

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

The Missing Sleigh Key is a free Christmas escape room you play in your browser. Crack five festive locks to find Santa’s lost key on Christmas Eve. It takes 20–30 minutes, suits 1–6 players aged 8+, and needs no download, printer or sign-up.

Ready to save Christmas?

Santa leaves at midnight, but his sleigh key is missing. The elves left a trail of five locked clues. Solve each one to unlock the next. Grab a pen and paper for working out.

How to play

  1. Press Start the game. The timer starts.
  2. Read the story and solve the puzzle. Type the answer and press Unlock.
  3. Stuck? Press Need a hint?
  4. Open all five locks, then make a certificate with your time.

What’s in the game

  1. Rudolph’s coded note: a Caesar shift code, where every letter has moved 3 places.
  2. Mrs Claus’s gift tags: a number code where A = 1, B = 2 and so on.
  3. The elf’s riddle: a rhyming riddle.
  4. The toy box dial: a Christmas maths lock.
  5. Santa’s poem: an acrostic poem, where the first letters spell the answer.

Playing over Zoom or FaceTime

One person shares their screen and reads the story aloud; everyone else shouts answers. Or send the link to each household, start together and compare finishing times at the end. It works well for grandparents who can’t travel.

Answers

Only peek if you’re really stuck.

  1. Lock 1: CHIMNEY – shift each letter back 3.
  2. Lock 2: STOCKING – A=1 number code.
  3. Lock 3: TREE – needles, baubles, presents underneath.
  4. Lock 4: 133 – 9 × 12 + 25.
  5. Lock 5: COOKIES – first letters of each line.

Frequently asked questions

How long does The Missing Sleigh Key take?

Most families finish in 20–30 minutes. Fast adult teams can finish in under 10 minutes; younger children may take 40 minutes with a grown-up helping.

What age is it for?

Ages 8 and up can play on their own. Children aged 5–7 can play with an adult reading the clues and helping with the maths lock.

Is there an answer key?

Yes. Each lock has a hint button, and the full answers are at the bottom of this page behind a ‘show answers’ button.

Can we play it on a phone?

Yes. The game works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers. Nothing to download.

Can we play over Zoom?

Yes. One person shares their screen and everyone calls out answers, or each household plays the link at the same time and compares finishing times.
