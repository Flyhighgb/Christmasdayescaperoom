Christmas escape room builder: make your own in 2 minutes

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

The free Christmas escape room builder makes a custom printable escape room for your home. Choose the players’ age, a story and 3–8 hiding places (tree, stocking, fridge…). It creates a clue card for each one, with puzzles that suit the age, plus a host sheet with every answer. It takes about 2 minutes.

For ages 3–5, every clue is a big picture of the next hiding place. Read the cards aloud.

Hiding places, in the order players will find them (pick 3–8)

The last place you tick is where the prize goes. Cards follow the list order above.

If nothing happened, press Ctrl + P or ⌘ + P. The host sheet prints first, then one clue card per page.

How the escape room builder works

  1. Choose the age. The builder only uses puzzles that suit it, from picture clues for toddlers to pigpen ciphers for adults.
  2. Pick a story and hiding places. Each clue’s answer is the next hiding place, so players move around your home.
  3. Print and hide. The host sheet tells you exactly where each card goes and every answer.
  4. Play. Hand over Card 1, start the timer, and finish with a certificate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I make my own Christmas escape room?

Use the free escape room builder: choose the players’ age, a story and 3–8 hiding places in your home. It creates printable clue cards where each answer leads to the next hiding place, plus a host sheet with every answer.

What puzzles does the builder use?

It picks puzzles that suit the age you choose: picture clues for ages 3–5; riddles, word scrambles, number codes and backwards messages for 6–8; plus Caesar shifts and pigpen ciphers for older children; and missing-vowel and backwards-alphabet codes for teens and adults.

Can I get different puzzles?

Yes. Press ‘Shuffle puzzles’ to make a new version with the same hiding places, which is handy if you play more than once or run two teams.

Is it free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up. Print as many games as you like for home, school or work.

How long will the game take?

Roughly 3 minutes per clue for ages 3–5, 6 minutes per clue for ages 6–8 and 8 minutes per clue for older players. Five hiding places makes a 30–40-minute game for most families.
