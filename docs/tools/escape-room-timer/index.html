Frequently asked questions

How long should a Christmas escape room timer be?

Set 30 minutes for children under 8, 45 minutes for families and 60 minutes for teenagers and adults.

Can I show the timer on the TV?

Yes. Open this page on a laptop connected to the TV, or cast your browser tab, then press Full screen.

How do hint penalties work?

Each time a team uses a hint, press ‘Hint used’. You can choose to add a 2-minute penalty, which takes time off the clock.
