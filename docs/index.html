How a home escape room works

  1. Choose a game to suit your group’s age and how much time you have.
  2. Play online straight away, or print and hide the clue cards (about 10 minutes).
  3. Start the timer and read the story out loud.
  4. Solve each clue. Every answer points to the next hiding place.
  5. Crack the final code to save Christmas, then print your certificate.

Frequently asked questions

What is a Christmas escape room?

A Christmas escape room is a festive puzzle game where players solve a chain of clues, codes and riddles against the clock to complete a mission, such as finding Santa’s lost key. At home you play with printed clue cards or online in a browser, with no real locked room.

Are the games on this site free?

Yes. Every game, printable and tool on christmasescaperoom.co.uk is free to play at home, in school or at work. There is no sign-up and no email needed to download.

How long does a Christmas escape room take?

Most of our games take 20–45 minutes. The online games take 20–30 minutes, family printables 30–45 minutes, the adults’ game Scrooge’s Safe 45–60 minutes, and the toddler game 15–20 minutes.

What age is a Christmas escape room for?

We have games for every age: Rudolph’s Lost Bells for ages 3–5, The Great Pudding Heist for 6+, Elf Academy Final Exam for 11–16, Scrooge’s Safe for adults, and Christmas Memory Lane for older adults in care homes.

Can I play a Christmas escape room without a printer?

Yes. The Missing Sleigh Key is played entirely online on a phone, tablet or laptop, so you don’t need a printer, props or a download.
