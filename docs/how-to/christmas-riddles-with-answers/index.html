30 Christmas riddles with answers (for kids, teens and adults)

By Paul, creator of Christmas Escape Room · Updated 22 November 2026

Here are 30 Christmas riddles with answers, sorted into easy (ages 5–8), medium (9–12) and hard (teens and adults). Tap a riddle to reveal its answer. Riddles whose answer is a place, such as a stocking or the tree, make perfect escape room clues.

Easy riddles for kids (ages 5–8)

1. I have needles but I never sew. I wear lights and baubles in a row. What am I?

Answer: A Christmas tree

2. I’m cold and white and fall from the sky. Roll me up and I’ll grow to be big. What am I?

Answer: Snow

3. I hang by the fireplace and get filled on Christmas Eve. What am I?

Answer: A stocking

4. I have a red nose that glows so bright, I help pull the sleigh on Christmas night. Who am I?

Answer: Rudolph

5. Pull me and I go bang! Inside you’ll find a joke and a paper hat. What am I?

Answer: A Christmas cracker

6. I’m round and shiny and hang on the tree. Drop me and I might break. What am I?

Answer: A bauble

7. I have a carrot for a nose and coal for eyes, and I melt when the sun comes out. Who am I?

Answer: A snowman

8. I’m wrapped in paper with a bow on top, and you open me on Christmas morning. What am I?

Answer: A present

9. I’m made of biscuit, I have buttons of sweets, and I live in a house you can eat. Who am I?

Answer: A gingerbread man

10. I’m tall when I’m new and short when I’m old, and I glow on the Christmas table. What am I?

Answer: A candle

Medium riddles for older kids (ages 9–12)

11. What can go up a chimney down, but can’t go down a chimney up?

Answer: An umbrella

12. What has bark but no bite, and stands in your living room in December?

Answer: A Christmas tree

13. I start with E, end with E, and usually contain only one letter. You send lots of me at Christmas.

Answer: An envelope

14. The more of me you make in the snow, the more you leave behind. What am I?

Answer: Footprints

15. What gets wetter the more it dries, especially after Christmas dinner washing-up?

Answer: A tea towel

16. What falls in winter but never gets hurt?

Answer: Snow

17. I have hands but can’t wrap presents, and I tell you when it’s time for bed on Christmas Eve. What am I?

Answer: A clock

18. I have one eye but I can’t see, and I help sew up a stocking. What am I?

Answer: A needle

19. What goes up every year but never comes down?

Answer: Your age

20. What can travel all the way to the North Pole while staying in the corner of an envelope?

Answer: A stamp

Hard riddles for teens and adults

21. Feed me and I live, give me a drink and I die. I keep you warm on Christmas Eve. What am I?

Answer: A fire

22. I can be cracked, made, told and played, and you’ll find one in every cracker. What am I?

Answer: A joke

23. What breaks as soon as you say its name? (Hint: think of a famous carol.)

Answer: Silence

24. I have cities but no houses, forests but no trees, and water but no fish. Santa needs me on Christmas Eve.

Answer: A map

25. What can you catch every winter but never throw?

Answer: A cold

26. What has many keys but can’t open a single lock, and plays carols?

Answer: A piano

27. If there are three mince pies and you take two, how many do you have?

Answer: Two

28. What has a neck but no head and wears a cork at Christmas?

Answer: A bottle

29. What has four legs but can’t walk, and everyone gathers round it for Christmas dinner?

Answer: A table

30. I’m full of holes but I still hold water, and I’m busy after Christmas dinner. What am I?

Answer: A sponge

Using riddles in a Christmas escape room

  1. Choose a riddle whose answer is a hiding place in your home.
  2. Write it on a card and hide the next clue in that place.
  3. Mix in a code from the Christmas cipher maker and finish with a prize.

Frequently asked questions

What are good Christmas riddles for an escape room?

Riddles with a hiding place as the answer work best: ‘I have needles but never sew’ (the tree), ‘I hang by the fireplace’ (a stocking) or ‘Pull me and I go bang’ (a cracker). Put the next clue in that place.

How do I use these riddles in an escape room?

Pick a riddle whose answer is an object in your home, write it on a card and hide the next clue there. Mix riddles with codes from our cipher maker for variety.

Are these riddles suitable for a classroom?

Yes. Use the easy set for KS1, the medium set for KS2 and the hard set for secondary school or staff quizzes.
